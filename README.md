## Things I made

- **[IMMORTALITY Clip Tracker](https://github.com/XxSeRyIxX/IMMORTALITY-Clip-Tracker)**: reads
  your IMMORTALITY save and tells you which clips you are still missing, and how to reach
  each one. [Open it](https://xxseryixx.github.io/IMMORTALITY-Clip-Tracker/)
- **[mail-cleaner](https://github.com/XxSeRyIxX/mail-cleaner)**: finds the senders that flood
  your inbox, unsubscribes and deletes everything they sent. Outlook, Gmail and IMAP.
- **[Stremio for Kodi](https://github.com/XxSeRyIxX/plugin.video.stremio2kodi)**: your Stremio
  add-ons inside Kodi.
- **[Emoji fonts for Kodi](https://github.com/XxSeRyIxX/script.emoji.fonts)**: adds Noto Emoji
  to your Kodi skin's fonts.
- **[My Kodi repository](https://github.com/XxSeRyIxX/kodi)**: installs and updates my add-ons
  from inside Kodi. [Open it](https://xxseryixx.github.io/kodi/)
- **[xxseryixx.github.io](https://github.com/XxSeRyIxX/xxseryixx.github.io)**: this profile as
